Sheet 03 / 06

Dwg. TM-2026-03

Section A-A — Ledger

A reconciliation console for a team of nine who close the books in four days. The drawn version had a dashboard. The built version does not: nobody opened it twice.

What shipped instead is a single table with a keyboard path through it, and a diff view that shows the two numbers side by side rather than the difference between them. Close time went from four days to a day and a half.

Sheet 04 / 06

Dwg. TM-2026-04

Detail 04 — Harbour

Shipment tracking for a freight forwarder. The constraint was a warehouse tablet in direct sun, held at arm's length, by someone wearing gloves. Every target on the built version is 44 pixels or larger, and the type never drops below 17.

The redline on this sheet is the one that mattered: the status colour alone failed on the tablet at noon, so status is now a printed word as well as a colour.
