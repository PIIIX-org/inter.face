Written account · 05.2

Atlas ERP is a multi-tenant system I build and ship. The plate opposite is its sign-in screen, photographed off the running build rather than drawn from memory, then printed to the sheet in the sheet's own two values.

Three fields. A company slug, an address, a secret. Everything else that a system this size wants to say on its first screen was cut, and cutting it is the part of the work that survives contact with a real tenant.

Nothing here is a mock. If the screen changes, the plate changes with it.
